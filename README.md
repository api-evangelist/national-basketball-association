# National Basketball Association (national-basketball-association)

The National Basketball Association (NBA) is the premier men's professional basketball league in North America, comprised of 30 franchises (29 in the United States and 1 in Canada). The NBA does not operate a public developer portal; statistical, scoring, and play-by-play data feeds are licensed through official partners (notably Sportradar and Genius Sports). The stats.nba.com endpoints are not officially documented as a public API.
